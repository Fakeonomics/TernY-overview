TernY

Ternary Transformer by Yuriy — tiny weights, trained end-to-end.
This is early research. The technology is not production-ready.
What it is

A ternary transformer that works

TernY is a transformer where every weight is ternary: -1, 0, or +1. No FP32 inference. No matrix multiplication in the traditional sense — just additions and subtractions. The model is trained end-to-end with latent FP32 weights and Straight-Through Estimator quantization (BitNet b1.58-style), then hardened to ternary at inference.

At 31M parameters, TernY v3 achieves perplexity 5.0 after 10 minutes of training on a laptop RTX 3050 (6 GB VRAM). It generates grammatically coherent English sentences. For a model that runs on ternary math, this is the starting line.

Architecture

How it works (briefly)

  • BitLinear — latent FP32 weights with absmean STE quantization to ternary. Per-channel FP16 scale.
  • SubLN — sublayer normalization (double LayerNorm: pre-attention + pre-FFN).
  • ReLU² — squared ReLU activation in the feed-forward network.
  • GQA + RoPE — Grouped Query Attention with Rotary Position Embeddings.
  • Tied embeddings — shared input/output embedding matrix (FP32).

The model follows the BitNet b1.58 paradigm: train in FP32 with quantization-aware training, then export to packed ternary for inference. No bias terms. No FP32 math at deployment.

This approach is distinct from earlier TernY versions (v1/v2) that used frozen random ternary weights. TernY v3 trains all weights in the latent space, then quantizes after training — achieving dramatically better results.
Limitations

What's still broken

Honest assessment. This is early-stage research. Many things are not there yet.
  • Small scale. 31M and 64M models. Not comparable to billion-parameter LMs.
  • Limited vocabulary. The BPE tokenizer was trained on a small corpus. Coherency degrades outside seen domains.
  • No hardware deployment. The model runs in Python. No C codegen, no MCU port, no accelerator backend yet.
  • No benchmark suite. Evaluated on loss/perplexity, not downstream tasks (MMLU, HellaSwag, etc.).
  • Training data. Curated mix, not a large-scale web corpus.
  • Model can hallucinate and invent words freely (the “cowle buine” problem).
Timeline

12 days from zero to working model

TernY was built from scratch in under two weeks. The trajectory: frozen random ternary (v1, day 1), scale-only training (v2, day 3), BitNet-style retthink (v3, day 7), working text generation (day 10), 64M model training (day 12+). Every iteration uncovered something that didn't work.

The v3 breakthrough (perplexity 5.0) was that training all weights in latent FP32 with STE quantization works, while frozen random ternary does not. This is the central lesson.

Status

What's next

TernY v3 (64M) is currently training on 82M tokens. Results expected within days. Beyond that, the roadmap includes: proper evaluation on standard benchmarks, C codegen for Cortex-M class MCUs, domain-layer fine-tuning (TernY-DL), and eventually a MatMul-Free recurrent variant (TernY-MF).
